OUR PROCESS

Our professional deep clean, step by step.

Pre-vacuuming. Targeted pre-spray. Spot and stain removal. TM3 CRB agitation. High-pressure hot-water extraction. Our complete five-stage deep clean.

A very different clean altogether.

The difference starts before the extraction wand touches your carpet. We professionally vacuum to remove loose soil, then apply a targeted pre-spray directly to the carpet, assess and treat suitable stains individually, and give the solutions time to work.

Next, the TM3 counter-rotating brush machine works through the pile, loosening dirt clinging to the fibres and lifting embedded soil towards the surface, ready for extraction.

Finally, we rinse and extract using fresh, clean water only through our high-pressure, high-temperature truck-mounted system. This flushes out the detergent and loosened soil, helping avoid sticky detergent residue and keeping your carpet cleaner for longer.

It is the combination of all five stages that makes this our most thorough clean. Targeted treatment for some stubborn stains is included. You can add antibacterial deodoriser or stain protection if you would like them.

01

SEBO BS360 vacuuming

A professional twin-motor vacuum, designed to remove as much loose dirt and debris as possible from your carpet before treatment begins.

02

Application of targeted pre-spray

Apply the appropriate pre-spray directly to the carpet, giving it time to loosen soil before the next stages.

03

Spot & stain removal

Assess individual spots and stains, then apply an appropriate spotter directly to each mark before extraction.

04
THE KEY STAGE OF THE DEEP CLEAN

TM3 CRB agitation

This is where the magic happens. The TM3 brushes through the pile to loosen dirt from the fibres and break down traffic-lane build-up. The loosened soil is then removed by the separate hot-water extraction stage.

05

High-pressure hot water extraction

We extract using fresh, clean water only. This rinses out the cleaning detergent and loosened soil, helping remove detergent residue so the carpet feels clean and is less prone to rapid re-soiling.
