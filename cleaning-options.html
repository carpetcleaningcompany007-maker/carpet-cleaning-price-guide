What’s included and who it suits

Detergent is mixed into the machine’s tank. The wand sprays the cleaning solution onto the carpet and extracts it as it passes over. The detergent used is self-neutralising and designed to remain in the carpet without a separate rinsing stage.

There’s no separate pre-spray, dwell time or mechanical agitation. It’s a very quick, more affordable process, similar to the wand-only cleans you often see advertised online at budget prices.

Targeted stain removal is not included. If your carpets have stains or are heavily soiled, this option may not be suitable.

A much more thorough process

This is a much more thorough process and a very different clean altogether. Our four-stage process prepares the carpet before thoroughly rinsing and extracting the loosened dirt and cleaning solution.

Targeted treatment for some stubborn stains and an antibacterial deodoriser are included. Optional stain protection is available at half price.

Please let us know beforehand about pet urine, paint or anything out of the ordinary, as these may require specialist treatment outside of the quoted clean.
