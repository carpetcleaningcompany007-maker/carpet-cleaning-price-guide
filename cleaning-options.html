The affordable option

Standard Wand Clean

£55 first room

£30 each additional room · £25 box room · £30 per set of stairs

Detergent is mixed into the machine’s tank. The wand sprays the cleaning solution onto the carpet and extracts it as it passes over.

The detergent used is self-neutralising and designed to remain in the carpet without a separate rinsing stage.

There’s no separate pre-spray, dwell time or mechanical agitation. It’s a very quick, more affordable process, similar to the wand-only cleans you often see advertised online at budget prices.

We offer this option for customers who want an affordable clean, while making clear what’s included.

Before you choose
Targeted stain removal is not included. If your carpets have stains or are heavily soiled, this option may not be suitable.
Our four-stage process

Professional Deep Clean

£75 first room

£45 each additional room · £25 box room · £45 per set of stairs

This is a much more thorough process and a very different clean altogether.

  1. Professional vacuumingRemoves loose dirt and debris before treatment begins.
  2. Targeted pre-sprayApplied directly to the carpet and given time to work on dirt and soiling.
  3. Counter-rotating brush agitationSeparates the fibres and loosens embedded dirt from deeper within the carpet.
  4. High-pressure hot water extractionHigh-temperature rinsing and extraction, powered by a £25,000 truck-mounted system, removes the loosened dirt and cleaning solution.
Included in your clean
Targeted treatment for some stubborn stains and an antibacterial deodoriser, free of charge. You can also choose to add stain protection at half price.
Please let us know beforehand about pet urine, paint or anything out of the ordinary, as these may require specialist treatment outside of the quoted clean.
