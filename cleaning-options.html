STAGE 01 / 04

Before any cleaning solution goes down, I professionally vacuum the carpet to remove loose dirt and debris.

PREPARE THE CARPET

Removing loose soil first gives the following stages a cleaner starting point.

Targeted pre-spray
STAGE 02 / 04

Give the cleaning solution time to work.

A specialist pre-spray is applied directly to the carpet and allowed time to work on the dirt and soiling. This is a separate treatment before extraction.

TREAT BEFORE RINSING

The solution has time to work, rather than being sprayed and immediately extracted.

CRB action illustrated
STAGE 03 / 04

Work deeper into the pile.

Two brushes rotate in opposite directions, working through the pile to separate the fibres and loosen embedded dirt from deeper within the carpet.

MECHANICAL PREPARATION

This extra machine work helps release soil that a wand-only clean may leave behind.

High-pressure hot water extraction
STAGE 04 / 04

Rinse it. Extract it. Remove it.

The carpet is thoroughly rinsed and extracted using high-pressure, high-temperature hot water extraction, powered by a £25,000 truck-mounted system.

REMOVE THE LOOSENED SOIL

The wand extracts the loosened dirt and cleaning solution after the preparation and agitation stages.

The affordable option

Standard Wand Clean

£55 first room

£30 each additional room · £25 box room · £30 per set of stairs

Detergent is mixed into the machine’s tank. The wand sprays the cleaning solution onto the carpet and extracts it as it passes over.

The detergent used is self-neutralising and designed to remain in the carpet without a separate rinsing stage.

There’s no separate pre-spray, dwell time or mechanical agitation. It’s a very quick, more affordable process, similar to the wand-only cleans you often see advertised online at budget prices.

We offer this option for customers who want an affordable clean, while making clear what’s included.

Before you choose
Targeted stain removal is not included. If your carpets have stains or are heavily soiled, this option may not be suitable.
