OUR PROCESS

This is our professional deep clean.

Professional vacuuming. Targeted pre-spray. TM3 CRB agitation. High-pressure hot water extraction. Our complete four-stage process.

See how this clean works

A very different clean altogether.

The difference starts before the extraction wand touches your carpet. We professionally vacuum to remove loose soil, then apply a targeted pre-spray directly to the carpet and give it time to work.

Next, the TM3 counter-rotating brush machine works through the pile, separating the fibres and loosening embedded dirt from deeper within the carpet, bringing it towards the surface ready for extraction.

We then rinse and extract using high-pressure, high-temperature hot water from our £25,000 truck-mounted system, often called steam cleaning. The system supplies water at up to 225°F at the machine; temperature at the carpet varies. This removes the loosened soil and cleaning solution after the preparation stages have done their work.

It is the combination of all four stages that makes this our most thorough clean. Targeted treatment for some stubborn stains and a free antibacterial deodoriser are included, with optional stain protection available at half price.

So, what’s the difference?

The standard clean

A budget wand clean: solution is sprayed onto the carpet and extracted as the wand passes over. There is no separate pre-spray, dwell time or mechanical brushing. It is a quicker refresh, with no targeted stain treatment.

The professional deep clean

The CRB is where the extra preparation makes a real difference. After vacuuming and pre-spray, its counter-rotating brushes work through the pile, loosening embedded dirt from deeper within the carpet and lifting it towards the surface.

High-pressure hot water extraction then rinses and removes that loosened soil. All four stages work together for our most thorough clean, with targeted treatment for some stubborn stains and free antibacterial deodoriser.
