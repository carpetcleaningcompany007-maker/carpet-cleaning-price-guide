Professional Deep Clean

OUR COMPLETE FOUR-STAGE PROCESS

A very different clean altogether.

We professionally vacuum, apply a targeted pre-spray and give it time to work. The TM3 counter-rotating brushes then work through the pile, separating the fibres and loosening embedded dirt from deeper within the carpet.

High-pressure, high-temperature hot water extraction, often called steam cleaning, then rinses and removes the loosened dirt and cleaning solution using our £25,000 truck-mounted system.

Every stage works towards the most thorough result we can achieve for your carpet. Targeted treatment for some stubborn stains and an antibacterial deodoriser are included.
